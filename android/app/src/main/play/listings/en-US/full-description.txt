Tutankhamun is an open-source re-implementation of the city-building game Pharaoh + Cleopatra 2.1 (2000), written again in C. It plays on the files of your own copy of the game: the same rules, the same look, and saved games that are compatible with the original.

THE GAME FILES ARE NOT INCLUDED. You need an installed Pharaoh + Cleopatra 2.1 (for example the GOG or Steam version, or an updated CD version). Copy the whole game folder (the one with Data, campaign.txt and Pharaoh_Text.eng) to your device, for example into Download, and choose it at the first start.

• The Pharaoh and Cleopatra campaigns and free play of every mission
• Saved games compatible with the original game
• Touch controls: tap to click, long press to right click, drag to scroll and to lay roads, walls and canals
• Tap to place a building's ghost, tap it again to build; a button cancels the building
• The on-screen keyboard for player names and saved games
• Landscape, full screen, on phones and tablets

The app collects no data and needs no internet connection.
