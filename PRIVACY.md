# Tutankhamun privacy policy

Tutankhamun (the Android app `com.github.dalerank.tutankhamun`, and the Windows, Linux and macOS programs) collects no
personal data. It sends something only when you send a bug report or an idea yourself.

- The report form (the button next to the mission briefing's) sends, when you press "Send", the title and the text
  you wrote, the game's build and system (for example "android"), the last part of the game's log and a screenshot
  of the game's screen. They go over HTTPS to https://playshhhit.com/tutankhamun, the developer's report service,
  which keeps them and publishes them as a public issue at https://github.com/dalerank/tutankhamun/issues. Do not
  write personal information into a report. To have a report removed, ask in its issue or write to
  dalerankn8@gmail.com.
- Apart from the reports the game makes no network connections of its own. It has no accounts, analytics,
  advertising or crash reporting of its own.
- It reads the files of the Pharaoh + Cleopatra folder that you choose yourself, and writes the game's players and
  saved games into that folder, as the original game does. Its settings and log stay on the device.
- Crash reports and statistics that Google Play collects for all apps are covered by Google's privacy policy; the
  developer sees them only in an anonymous form.
- On the desktop, when Discord runs on the same computer, the game shows the current mission in your Discord status
  through Discord's local connection; `config/discord.json` turns this off. The Android app does not do this.
- The Windows program checks https://github.com/dalerank/tutankhamun/releases for a newer build of itself.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/dalerank/tutankhamun/issues.

Last change: 26 November 2026.
