# Tutankhamun privacy policy

Tutankhamun (the Android app `com.github.dalerank.tutankhamun`, and the Windows, Linux and macOS programs) collects no
personal data and sends nothing anywhere.

- The app has no internet permission and makes no network connections. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising
  or crash reporting of its own.
- It reads the files of the Pharaoh + Cleopatra folder that you choose yourself, and writes the game's players and
  saved games into that folder, as the original game does. Its settings and log stay in the app's own storage on the
  device.
- Crash reports and statistics that Google Play collects for all apps are covered by Google's privacy policy; the
  developer sees them only in an anonymous form.
- On the desktop, when Discord runs on the same computer, the game shows the current mission in your Discord status
  through Discord's local connection; `config/discord.json` turns this off. The Android app does not do this.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/dalerank/tutankhamun/issues.

Last change: 7 November 2026.
